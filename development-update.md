# The story mode still has some explaining to do

November 11, 2026

I've been working on a slower story mode for Final Empires, starting around Rome. People should actually travel, gather resources, build and fight. Apparently getting all of those people to do their jobs is also my job now.

The last few rounds of testing have made the problems pretty obvious. There has been too much text covering the map, controls that take too much figuring out, and units that don't make their purpose clear. Saving and sound have also caused trouble. Yes, the menu music playing over the story music is a bug. Two soundtracks were not the plan.

## What I'm working through

- A cleaner, consistent interface. Keep the map visible, keep the build options easy to reach, and stop throwing long messages over everything.
- Workers that finish a gathering trip, carry a sensible amount home, and can be assigned without making the player fight the menus.
- Faster scouts that explore without taking ownership of every place they walk through.
- Armies that are easier to deploy and command.
- Reliable saving and loading, sound that can actually be unmuted, and less lag when discovering land.
- Marketplace tools for adding more maps and story worlds later.

There are changes in development, but I'm not calling the update finished yet. The next check is playing through it on a phone-shaped landscape screen, looking at what actually happens, and fixing what still gets in the way.

The game source stays private. I'll keep the public notes here focused on the problems, what changes, and what has actually been tested.
